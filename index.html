Privacy Policy — Soccer Powerhouse

Effective date: 27 September 2026

Developer: Digital MainFrame
Contact: digitalmainframe8080@gmail.com

Soccer Powerhouse ("the App") is a football trivia game. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Soccer Powerhouse does not require an account or sign-in.

The App does not ask for or collect your:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Physical address
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Precise location

We do not require you to provide personal information to play the game.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Your game progress is stored locally on your Android device. This includes information such as:

  • Power Points
  • Level and rank
  • Badges
  • Streaks
  • Questions you have answered
  • Game and app settings

This information is not transmitted to the developer.

Uninstalling the App or using the Reset Progress option deletes locally stored game progress.

3. Advertising

Soccer Powerhouse is free to use and is supported by advertising provided through Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect and process information associated with advertising and ad delivery, which may include information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from network information
  • Device and app information
  • Information about advertising interactions
  • Information used to detect and prevent invalid activity and fraud

The information collected by Google may be used to provide, measure, personalize, and improve advertising and related services, subject to Google's policies and applicable law.

Where required, Soccer Powerhouse uses Google's consent-management mechanisms to obtain your consent before applicable advertising requests are made. You can review or change applicable advertising privacy choices through Settings → Ad privacy choices within the App.

For more information, please see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

4. Gameplay Analytics

The App contains a local gameplay analytics component used to help understand gameplay activity and improve game balance.

In the current version of Soccer Powerhouse, these gameplay events are processed locally on the device. The App does not send these gameplay analytics to the developer or to a third-party analytics server.

You can enable or disable gameplay analytics through the App's Settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Soccer Powerhouse is not directed specifically at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The App does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information.

6. Data Security

Game progress is stored in the App's private Android storage.

Advertising-related network communication is handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK using appropriate network security mechanisms.

7. Third-Party Services

Soccer Powerhouse uses Google AdMob to provide advertising.

Google may process information according to its own privacy policies and terms. We encourage users to review Google's privacy documentation for information about how Google handles advertising-related data.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, services, or data practices change.

When we make changes, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this policy.
